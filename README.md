# Analysis
Amber's Braids is a small hair business based in Garankuwa, Pretoria. The business is owned and ran by Amber Koketso and focuses on protective hairstyles for natural hair.

# Goals and Objectives.
The main goal of the website is to attract more customers, increase the visibility of Amber's Braids and help customers get services easier.

# Target Audience
The primary target audience is women between the ages of 15 to 35 who are looking for neat and long-lasting hairstyles. All age groups are allowed, and men are allowed to braid too.

# Website Structure
The website will consist of 5 required pages which are the Homepage, About us, Services, Enquiry, and Contact Page.

# Budget
The domain name used is .co.za and it costs R150 per year. Web hosting will cost R600 per year which is R50 per month making the total costs R750 per year.
